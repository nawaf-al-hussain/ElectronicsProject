# Curated Project Problems — teacher's problem menu (verbatim extraction)

*(Source: `../sources/curated-project-problems.docx` — the course's suggested directions.)*

## Direction 1 — Sensor-based data acquisition, storage, analysis and visual dashboard

> Like: patient monitoring BPM, SpO2 etc over the whole day, saving them in an SD card, sending them to a cloud server, pulling from a doctor's PC and a dashboard there (Python Streamlit etc).

> Energy harvesting by wind-turbine, solar panels, piezo-electric materials in different environments and meeting a good percentage of energy demand there.

> A scenario may be: solar panels on passenger sheds where passengers wait for the bus, piezoelectric floor tiles and a wind turbine on the bus roof feeding bus fan and lighting loads.

## Direction 2 — Motion and Robotics (IMU sensors)

> Self-balancing monocycle, robotic arms, drones, ornithopters, cars, CNC machines.
