## Read-through for project selection

1. **Agriculture is the biggest single cluster (10 of 36 = 28%).** A generic 'smart farming / irrigation' pitch lands in the most crowded lane of the show.
2. **Three teams already build multi-operation agricultural robots with mobile control** — the same top-level concept as the CropGuard dossier. Differentiation must come from the *operations* (seedling transplanting, mechanical weeding, buzz-pollination), the *measured reliability* (8/10 gates, live counters), and the *engineering process evidence* (priced BOM, power budget), not from 'we made an agri robot'.
3. **Rescue rovers are the #2 cluster (5 entries, 4 distinct)** — also saturated.
4. **Rare lanes this season:** energy *harvesting* (0 entries — wind/piezo/solar harvesting from the curated problems list is untouched), CNC/pen-plotter (0), mesh communication (1), 3D scanning/instrumentation (1), medicine vending (1).
5. Rubric implication (verified from the official guidelines): Features & Hardware Implementation = 40% — the four-operation platform architecture is aimed exactly at the heaviest criterion.
